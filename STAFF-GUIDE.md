# How to Edit Your Website — KBCF Staff Guide

No coding, ever. Everything on the site is edited in the **Studio** — a private
admin page at:

> **kingdombuilderscf.org/studio** (or the preview URL + `/studio` before launch)

Sign in with the church account you were invited with. Changes go live about a
minute after you press **Publish**.

**The two rules that matter:**
1. Nothing is public until you press the green **Publish** button.
2. You can't break the website from the Studio. Worst case, use the document's
   **history** (clock icon, top right) to restore an earlier version.

## Update service times (or phone, address, socials)

1. In the left list, click **Site Settings**.
2. Under **Service times**, click an entry to change its day, label, or time —
   or **Add item** for a new gathering. The Prayer Line entry also holds the
   dial-in number and passcode.
3. **Publish.** The times update everywhere at once — the bar under the homepage
   hero, the footer, and the Contact page.

*[SCREENSHOT: Site Settings → Service times list]*

## Add a sermon

1. Left list → **Sermons** → **＋** (new document).
2. Fill in: **Title**, **Slug** (click "Generate"), **Date**, **Speaker** (pick
   from the list), **Series** (optional), **Scripture references**, and a short
   **Description**.
3. Add the **Video URL** (YouTube or Facebook link) *or* the BoxCast broadcast ID.
4. Upload a **Thumbnail** photo and type what's in the picture in "Alt text".
5. **Publish.** It appears at the top of the Watch page automatically.

## Post an event

1. Left list → **Events** → **＋**.
2. **Title**, **Slug** (Generate), **Start** date & time, **Location**,
   short **Description**. If sign-ups happen elsewhere, paste the link in
   **Registration URL** — otherwise leave it blank.
3. **Publish.** It shows on the Events page (list *and* calendar), and the soonest
   upcoming event automatically becomes the announcement strip on the homepage
   whenever no banner is set (see "Change the banner" below).

*[SCREENSHOT: New event form]*

## Edit Dream Center programs

1. Left list → **Outreach Programs**. Each card on the Dream Center page is one
   document (Feeding Families, Back to School Bash, …).
2. Open one to change its **description**, **stat** (e.g. "500+"), **stat label**
   (e.g. "households · 25,000+ lbs of food weekly"), or **schedule**
   (e.g. "Thursdays 11 AM").
3. **Publish.** Numbers on the page update immediately — keep them honest and
   current; they're the church's public impact record.

## Change the homepage banner

1. Left list → **Home Page** → the **Event banner** tab.
2. Type the **title** (e.g. "Corporate Prayer & Bible Study"), a **date**
   (e.g. "August 14, 2026"), the **location**, and optionally a button label + link.
3. **Publish.**

Good to know:
- The banner **hides itself the day after its date passes** — no stale announcements.
- If you turn it off (the "Show banner" switch) or leave it empty, the site shows
  the soonest upcoming event there instead.

*[SCREENSHOT: Home Page → Event banner fields]*

## Publish a blog post

1. Left list → **Blog Posts** → **＋**.
2. **Title**, **Slug** (Generate), **Date**, your name as **Author**, an
   **Excerpt** (the 1–2 sentence preview), a **Cover image** with alt text, and
   the article itself in **Body** (it works like a simple Word document —
   headings, bold, lists).
3. **Publish.**

## Photos, everywhere

Any image field: click **Upload**, choose the photo, then **always fill in
"Alt text"** — one sentence describing the picture. It's read aloud to blind
visitors and required before things look right. Landscape photos ~1600px wide
work best; portraits ~800×800.

## Things the Studio does NOT control

- **Sermon clips** — created and published by the clip tool automatically; they
  appear on the Watch page on their own. (You *can* open **Clips** in the Studio
  to fix a typo in a hook or unpublish one: set Status back to "scheduled".)
- **Online giving** — the Give button goes to the church's Clover page; changing
  giving providers is a request to your web person.
- **The live stream** — comes from BoxCast; go live there as usual and the site
  picks it up.

## If something looks wrong

1. Open the document you edited → clock icon (top right) → pick an earlier
   version → **Restore**, then **Publish**.
2. Still stuck? Nothing you did is permanent — email your web person with the
   page name and what you expected to see.
